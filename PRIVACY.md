# Privacy policy: CardComp

Last updated: 16 October 2026

CardComp ("CardComp: Find the Best Pakistani Card For You") compares bank card discounts in Pakistan. It is a website (https://cardcomp.hnaveed.me) and a Chrome extension.

**What it stores.** Your settings (city, the cards you picked, your typical bill, the places you starred, visit counts and any annual fees you typed) and a cache of discount data. All of this is stored in your browser: in the website's browser storage, or in Chrome's extension storage for the extension. In the extension, if you have turned on Chrome sync, your starred places, visit counts, bills per place and typed annual fees are also synced through your Google account to your other Chrome browsers, the same way Chrome syncs your other extension data. Nothing is ever sent to the developer or to anyone else.

**What it sends.** To show discounts, CardComp requests public discount data from peekaboo.guru: the list of banks, cards, categories and places in your city, and the deals at a place. These requests contain your chosen city, the card and category you are looking at, and place names you search for. They contain no personal information. peekaboo.guru's own privacy policy applies to those requests.

**What it does not do.** No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no ads, and no selling or sharing of data. It does not read the pages you visit. The website uses no cookies.

**Removing your data.** On the website, clear the site data for cardcomp.hnaveed.me in your browser settings. Uninstalling the extension deletes what it stored in that browser. To delete synced data from your Google account, use "Clear data" at https://chrome.google.com/sync.
